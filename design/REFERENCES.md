Read-only mobile (390px) and desktop (1440px) inspections were made on 2026-10-20 using reduced-motion preferences. No forms were submitted. Media and non-GET requests were blocked. Motion-dependent reference sites sometimes left incomplete first-screen visuals; those limitations are noted rather than treating a blank capture as a design recommendation.
