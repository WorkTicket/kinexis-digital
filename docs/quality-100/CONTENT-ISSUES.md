- Pricing and plan inclusions: reviewed against canonical catalog; see `COMMERCIAL-CONSISTENCY.md`.
- Guarantee, cancellation, ownership, domain, tax, and add-on language: reviewed on pricing/terms and shared content.
- Portfolio/case studies: qualitative project descriptions; no unsupported revenue/conversion figures identified in case-study data.
- English/Spanish: pricing, plan names, preview CTAs, cancellation, ownership, guarantee, and text-back rules represented in both; Spain euro display is a documented localization convention.

## No-change decision

No public copy was altered. All identified discrepancies either concern actual commercial policy/payment timing or evidence that requires owner confirmation. Changing those without approval would risk inventing terms or suppressing legitimate trust claims.
