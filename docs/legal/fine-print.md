**House rules for fine print** (FTC .com Disclosures; 16 CFR §251.1; ROSCA)
1. **Next to the claim, same format.** If the price is on the image, the minimum goes on the image too. Don't rely only on primary text, because Meta cuts it off after a few lines ("…See more"), and Stories show even less.
2. **Readable at phone size:** at least ~24 px on a 1080-px-wide image, with good contrast. No grey-on-grey.
3. **The terms URL is for detail, not for material terms.** Price, minimum, renewal and guarantee conditions must be visible without clicking.
4. **The same words everywhere:** "tracked lead", "3-month minimum", "after carrier approval". In Spanish: "contacto registrado", never "cliente registrado" (a "cliente" is a paying customer, which invites guarantee disputes).
5. **No unsubstantiated claims:** no "every call", no "local/Houston-based". "★ Most popular" stays on the Lead System by owner decision (owner reports 2 clients); keep it only while that is true of the Lead System.
6. **kinexisdigital.com/terms must be live before any ad that references it runs.**
