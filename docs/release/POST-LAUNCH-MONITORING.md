# Post-launch monitoring

**Status: NOT APPLICABLE YET — production is not deployed.**

After separately authorized deployment, assign named on-call owners and approved read-only access for the first 24 hours and first 7 days. Monitor availability, Worker errors, D1 and notification failures, payment/webhook errors, chatbot, consent/analytics, security events, and field Core Web Vitals. Separate labeled test traffic from genuine prospects; do not expose personal data in reports.

Record alert thresholds, review cadence, escalation contacts, and rollback decision authority before launch. Do not claim post-launch health or business impact without observed evidence.
