1. Free homepage preview

Every plan starts with a free homepage preview. No card and no obligation. Free homepage previews are delivered within 2 business days of receiving your business details. We will show you a homepage concept for your business. You can approve it, ask for changes, or walk away without paying.

2. Monthly plans

Website is $97/mo. Lead System is $197/mo. Plus (Bilingual) is $297/mo. All three monthly plans are $0 down and begin on launch day.

Each monthly plan is month-to-month from launch. There is no long-term contract and no cancellation fee. You may cancel anytime by text, email, or Messenger.

Your monthly price is locked for 24 months from launch. After 24 months we will give you at least 30 days’ notice before any price change.

3. What is included

Page counts and included work are listed on the pricing page for each plan. An extra page on Website is $99. Lead System includes one new page a month, and Plus includes two. Unused page allowances do not roll over.

A small edit is a change that takes 30 minutes or less, handled one at a time. Website includes up to two small edits a month in two business days. Lead System includes unlimited small edits in one business day. Plus includes unlimited small edits by the same business day when requested by noon Central.

Hosting, SSL, security and backups are included while a monthly plan is active. Domain year 1 is included on every monthly plan and on Own It, registered in your name; later renewals are billed at cost. On Own It, hosting and SSL are included for the first 12 months; optional Site Care is $39/mo after that.

4. Texting and carrier approval

Missed-call text-back is optional and needs a dedicated business phone line. On Lead System or Plus, a second tracking number is $1/mo if you use one, plus carrier text rates. On Website or Own It, text-back can be added for $97/mo.

Google review requests are sent by text after completed jobs on Lead System and Plus. Missed-call text-back is a separate optional feature. Texting for both features is subject to carrier approval, which typically takes 10–15 days and is not guaranteed. Messaging rates or carrier requirements may change.

5. 60-day lead guarantee

Lead System and Plus only. If you have zero tracked leads in your first 60 days live, we waive your 3rd and 4th monthly charges. One guarantee per business, and the account must be in good standing.

A tracked lead is a call of 30+ seconds, a missed caller who replies to the text-back, or a real quote form. Spam, wrong numbers, job applicants, vendors and existing customers do not count. The guarantee does not apply if tracking is unavailable because of a change you make, or if you pause or cancel the plan before day 60.

Own It does not include a lead guarantee.

6. Own It

Own It is a one-time $1,497 payment, due when you approve your preview. There is no monthly plan. The site files are yours from launch. Hosting and SSL are included for 12 months, the domain is registered for year one, and small edits are included for 12 months up to 30 minutes per month.

Own It small edits are done within 2 business days, and unused edit time does not roll over. After the first 12 months, hosting and edits are optional through Site Care at $39/mo for hosting and up to 30 minutes of edits a month. Domain renewals after year one are at cost. New pages are quoted in writing before work starts. Missed-call text-back can be added to Own It for $97/mo (see section 4).

7. Cancellation and access

Cancel a monthly plan anytime by text, email, or Messenger. There is no cancellation fee. Billing stops at the end of the period you have already paid for, and your site stays live through that period.

Because the site is hosted and managed by Kinexis on monthly plans, when the paid period ends, hosting and management stop and the site comes offline unless you keep it another way.

Buyout: a written one-time $1,497 purchase of the site files so you can move them to hosting you control. Ask us for buyout terms in writing before you cancel if that is the path you want.

Move to Own It: switch from a monthly plan to the Own It one-time purchase before your paid period ends. Own It terms (files, included hosting, and Site Care) are in section 6.

If you do not arrange a buyout or move to Own It before the paid period ends, the site comes offline when hosting and management stop.

8. Taxes and third-party costs

Prices are in U.S. dollars and exclude applicable taxes. Domain renewals, paid stock images, paid advertising and other third-party services are not included unless we list them in writing.

9. Questions

Text or call (307) 500-3371 or email hello@kinexisdigital.com. We will answer plan questions before you pay.

10. Refund requests during the first 30 days

If you are not satisfied with your website within 30 days of launch, we will refund your most recent payment in full. The guarantee applies to monthly plans and Own It. Requests must be made in writing by text, email or Messenger within 30 days of launch. Refunds do not apply to third-party costs (including domain renewals, paid stock images, paid advertising or other third-party services) that we have already paid on your behalf.
